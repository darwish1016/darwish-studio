FAQ

What do you need from me to get started?

A short description of your business, who your customers are, any brand assets you already have (logo, colours, photos) and a few websites you like. We’ll go through the rest together on a first call.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages and features you need. After our first call I’ll send you a clear quote, so you know the cost before any work starts.

Will I be able to update the website myself?

Yes. I build in WordPress with Elementor, or in Shopify, and show you how to change text, images and offers without breaking the layout.

Do you work with clients outside the Philippines?

Yes. Most of my clients are in Australia, the UK and the US. I plan my day around your working hours, so calls and replies happen when you need them.

Can you work with my existing brand?

Of course. I’ll follow your logo, colours and tone, and if you don’t have brand guidelines yet, I can create simple ones that match your website.
