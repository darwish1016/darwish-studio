The problem

Lots of services, one first impression

Callaughan Partners helps individuals, businesses and investors with accounting, tax, bookkeeping and advisory. That’s a broad offer to fit on one homepage.

Potential clients needed to see quickly what the firm does, feel confident in its expertise, and know exactly how to start a conversation.

The goal

What it had to do

  1. Present a broad range of services clearly.
  2. Build trust with real client reviews.
  3. Make booking a first meeting easy.

How I worked

Calm design for serious work

Money is stressful, so the design is deliberately calm: a confident navy palette, generous spacing and short, reassuring copy.

I focused on the order of the page: who the firm helps, how it helps, what clients say, and then a clear invitation to talk.

The build

The decisions that mattered

  1. 01

    A calm, confident palette

    Deep navy and crisp white feel professional without being cold.

  2. 02

    Proof up front

    A Google reviews section lets real clients do the convincing.

  3. 03

    A clear invitation

    A “Let’s plan your financial future with confidence” band closes the page with one obvious next step.

The result

Polished, and easy to trust

Callaughan Partners now has a polished website that matches the quality of its advice, and makes it easy for new clients to take the first step.
