The problem

A new idea needs a clear explanation

Behavioural profiling sounds abstract until you’ve tried it. Visitors needed to understand what Jigsaw Discovery is, how a session works and why it helps teams, before they’d think about enquiring.

The goal

What it had to do

  1. Explain the program in seconds, without jargon.
  2. Show the real people who run the sessions.
  3. Make sending an enquiry quick and simple.

How I worked

Let the idea speak for itself

The program uses physical jigsaw pieces to build a picture of how each person works, so I leaned into that: a bold red, a playful mask made of puzzle pieces, and short, confident headlines.

From there, the page walks visitors through the experience step by step, then introduces the team behind it before asking them to get in touch.

The build

The decisions that mattered

  1. 01

    A bold first impression

    A strong red hero and the jigsaw mask make the program memorable from the first second.

  2. 02

    The story in small steps

    Short sections explain what happens in a session and why it sticks, one idea at a time.

  3. 03

    Real faces build trust

    “The team behind the experience” puts people before the enquiry form, so reaching out feels personal.

The result

Memorable, and easy to enquire

Jigsaw Discovery now has a home that feels as energetic as the sessions themselves, with a clear path from “what is this?” to “let’s book one”.
