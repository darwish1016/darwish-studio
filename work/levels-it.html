The problem

Great service, hard to explain

Levels IT covers a lot: managed IT, cloud and internet, phone systems, cyber security and web development. Business owners shopping for IT support don’t want acronyms. They want to know their business will keep running.

The website had to explain a wide range of technical services without overwhelming anyone, and give visitors an easy first step.

The goal

What it had to do

  1. Explain every service in plain language a business owner understands.
  2. Show clear packages and prices, so visitors can tell what fits them.
  3. Turn visitors into free IT assessment bookings.

How I worked

Translate tech into everyday words

I worked closely with the Levels IT team on the website, SEO, email and social media. For the site, I rewrote the structure around what a business owner worries about, not how an IT team organises itself.

Services became simple photo cards with short summaries, and the packages became clear plans with a monthly price, so visitors can compare options without booking a call first.

The build

The decisions that mattered

  1. 01

    A free assessment as the front door

    The hero offers a free IT assessment straight away, a low-risk way to start a conversation.

  2. 02

    Packages people can compare

    Plans like “Small Business: Complete IT Management” show who they’re for and what they cost.

  3. 03

    Built to be found

    Clear headings, page titles and lightweight images give search engines and visitors the same easy read.

The result

Clearer, and easier to find

Levels IT now has a modern website that explains its services clearly and invites the next step on every page. Together with the SEO, email and social media work, it helped improve Levels IT’s digital visibility by over 80%.
